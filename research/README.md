## Research lanes

1. Philosophy: self-interest, self-authorship, chosen meaning. Explain propositions and permit disagreement.
2. Behaviour: assertiveness, approval pressure, follow-through and recovery. Use original, practical exercises.
3. Learning: recall, distributed practice, feedback and transfer. Check retention separately from real-world application.
4. Reasoning: assumptions, uncertainty, incentives, consequences and safeguards. Explain when each model fails.
5. Value creation: audience evidence, offers, iteration and repeatable delivery. Treat our commercial exercises as design hypotheses until piloted.

## Claim rules

Founder doctrine is a declared position. A mental model is a tool, not a law of the universe. A research finding has a population, method and limits. A programme hypothesis needs testing. A testimonial reports an individual's experience and is not a guarantee.

Do not claim all successful people share one psychology; that altruism universally destroys people; that declarations make external events true; or that six weeks produces a validated permanent transformation. Teach the agency and behaviours the founder wants without inventing evidence.
