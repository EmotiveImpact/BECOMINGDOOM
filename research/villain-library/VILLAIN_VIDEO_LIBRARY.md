## V01 expanded: Doctor Doom | Marvel 101

**Sources:** VP02

**Inspected evidence:** Creator interview on the 2019–2020 Doom series; full public text inspected. See VP02 for bounded evidence summary and exact question locator.

**Attribution boundary:** This is primary evidence about Cantwell’s characterization, not a transcript of Marvel 101 and not a psychological finding.

**Original teaching analysis:** Ask what would distinguish genuine improvement from a convincing performance of improvement. A declaration such as becoming more disciplined needs a behavior, an occasion to demonstrate it and a way to notice failure. The learner chooses one recurring task, states its completion criterion and identifies a person or artifact that can reveal whether it was done. A self-description is allowed to motivate action, but cannot count as the evidence of action. An adverse result prompts a changed plan rather than an attack on the messenger.

**Observable task proof:** A dated work artifact with an agreed acceptance criterion; a written revision after feedback.

**Discussion:** Would I still want this improvement if nobody praised me for it?

## V02 expanded: Why Is It Impossible To Get Doctor Doom Right?

**Sources:** VP02; VP09

**Inspected evidence:** Two directly inspected creator interviews: Cantwell on Doom’s failed change; North on the construction of Doom’s voice. Their bounded evidence summaries are in VP02 and VP09.

**Attribution boundary:** These are independent contextual sources, not evidence that Nerdstalgic makes the same arguments. The two writers discuss different runs.

**Original teaching analysis:** Distinguish the dramatic appeal of certainty from the demands of good judgment. A character’s concise speeches are selected and edited; a learner’s real decisions arrive with incomplete information. The useful transfer is preparation: write the actual decision, identify missing facts, and decide when enough evidence exists to act. Disciplined confidence includes an uncertainty statement. It should not require performing omniscience, refusing questions or equating hesitation with weakness. This also makes the Doom metaphor more durable: retain deliberate preparation while declining an impossible standard of fictional perfection.

**Observable task proof:** One decision memo with known facts, uncertainties, a deadline and a reversible first step.

**Discussion:** Which part of this identity is an actionable standard, and which is simply good theater?

## V04 expanded: Ryan North talks ONE WORLD UNDER DOOM, Fantastic Four & more!

**Sources:** VP03; VP09

**Inspected evidence:** Official One World Under Doom premise plus an independently hosted Ryan North interview with a public time-coded text. VP09 identifies the exact text segments inspected.

**Attribution boundary:** The text belongs to the Cryptid Creator Corner interview, not the Owen Likes More Comics video. Text time labels have not been checked against playback.

**Original teaching analysis:** Use two tests for an apparently beneficial plan. First, would it produce the promised benefit? Second, who can challenge it, reject it or change its administrator? These are different questions, and neither can be answered merely by the leader’s confidence. In a course exercise, choose a small team improvement and identify the affected people, its downside and a method for getting meaningful consent. Build a way for others to continue the work without dependence on one indispensable individual. This turns the attraction of competent leadership into accountable practice.

**Observable task proof:** A proposal recording beneficiaries, costs, consent, independent feedback and an exit or rollback condition.

**Discussion:** Does the plan still work if someone else leads it or an affected person says no?

## V09 expanded: Why Thanos Changed – Wisecrack Edition

**Sources:** VP06; VP07; VP10

**Inspected evidence:** A Wisecrack contributor’s own 2018 article was read in full; VP10 records its scarcity argument and treatment of competing interpretations.

**Attribution boundary:** Theriault wrote a different Wisecrack QuickTake. V09 credits Joshua Corin; do not attribute Theriault’s essay to Corin or call it a companion transcript for V09.

**Original teaching analysis:** Treat a compelling explanation as a hypothesis, then compare feasible means. For a practical project, identify the limiting resource, evidence that it is limiting, and at least two alternatives to the first proposed remedy. The important test is whether the remedy addresses the mechanism or merely makes the decision-maker feel decisive. Add an explicit prohibition on sacrificing other people’s basic rights for an asserted aggregate benefit. A false dilemma often disappears once assumptions about resources, timing or cooperation are made visible.

**Observable task proof:** An assumption table with evidence, alternatives, foreseeable harms and a test that could disconfirm the preferred plan.

**Discussion:** What evidence would make me abandon the solution I currently feel most certain about?

## V10 expanded: The Philosophy of Thanos: Marvel’s Conflicted Nihilist

**Sources:** VP06; VP07; VP10

**Inspected evidence:** Official comics/screen context and the fully inspected Theriault article provide separate grounds for comparing motives, values and consequences.

**Attribution boundary:** The new article is not by NerdSync. It supplies a counterpoint and a method for testing interpretations, not a verified account of this video.

**Original teaching analysis:** Avoid treating a philosophical label as a conclusion. Instead state a proposition in ordinary words, identify the value it assumes and ask how a concrete action follows from it. A person may reject external approval while still caring deeply about their work, relationships or promises; that need not be a contradiction. The exercise should therefore test actual propositions rather than attach a dramatic label to the learner. Record what matters, what action would express it, and what boundary prevents that action from overriding another person’s equally important interests.

**Observable task proof:** A personal commitment expressed as a concrete action plus a constraint protecting others.

**Discussion:** Can I explain this choice without a philosophical label, and does the explanation survive a counterexample?
