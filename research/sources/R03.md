## What the inspected material supports

Rand distinguishes rational self-interest from arbitrary desire and rejects equating it with sacrificing others.

## Proposed course application

Frame self-interest as an intentional, reasoned ethical position. This application is our design decision, not an exercise claimed to come verbatim from the source.

## Limits

Contested normative philosophy, not empirical proof that selfishness causes success. Course need not adopt Objectivism wholesale.
