### 1. Assertiveness: strengthen practice, keep the claims narrow

**BDS-20261024-01.** Hede, Malouff and Meynadier. *A Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials on the Efficacy of Assertiveness Training for Social Anxiety*. International Journal of Applied Positive Psychology. Published **28 May 2026**. Peer-reviewed review. [Original article / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41042-026-00297-7).

**Scientific finding and appraisal.** Twelve RCTs, 503 participants, mixed clinical/educational populations: social-anxiety benefit g = 0.62, 95% CI 0.32–0.92; I² = 56.72%. Outcomes were self-reported, not observed boundary behaviour. Only three studies supplied relevant follow-up; their durations were not individually verified here. Randomisation methods were largely unreported; four studies omitted attrition rates. The bespoke quality checklist leaves concerns about bias. Publication-bias tests were negative but small evidence sets cannot exclude it. The paper describes two GRADE downgrades from high, yet labels certainty moderate: an unresolved reporting inconsistency. Our assessment is cautious, not a formal regrading. Multiple trials support the signal, but this does not establish durable, transferable agency or superiority to established therapies.

**Practical implication, our inference.** The existing week-one rehearsal deserves evaluation using what a participant actually says and does. A reduction in felt anxiety and successful handling of a real request are different outcomes. Do not require the former before attempting the latter.

**Original Doom application, unvalidated.** Proposed doctrine: **“A boundary is a decision you can enact under pressure.”** Add a Boundary Under Pressure card to W01-D03, then revisit it during W02-D06:
1. Name the chosen priority and the obligation that must still be honoured.
2. Write one clear limit and a feasible alternative only if one is genuinely available.
3. Rehearse a reasonable objection with a partner.
4. Try an appropriate low-stakes conversation and record the action, outcome and revision.

This builds on existing curriculum work rather than adding another daily assignment. The “Pressure Check” is a design checklist: can I state the limit, recognise a legitimate obligation, and revise without automatically surrendering the priority? It is not a psychological instrument, and no summed personality score should be created.

### 2. Attention: test access conditions and feasibility

**BDS-20261024-02.** Castelo, Kushlev, Ward, Esterman and Reiner. *Blocking mobile internet on smartphones improves sustained attention, mental health, and subjective well-being*. PNAS Nexus. Published **18 February 2025**. Peer-reviewed RCT; older evidence newly appraised. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf017) | [Open original manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11834938/).

**Scientific finding and appraisal.** Preregistered delayed-treatment trial: 467 US/Canadian iPhone users; two-week blocking periods, four-week study. Objective attention improved; the time-by-condition interaction was p = .016. Pooled attention change d_z = .23 is within-person, not a randomised between-group effect; no CI for that estimate is supplied in the main-text result. Wellbeing measures were self-reported. Only 119/467 met adherence criteria; 313 completed all surveys. Primary analyses used complete cases despite the “intention-to-treat” label. Motivated volunteers, attrition and an inactive control limit inference. The delayed arm reproduced the pattern within the same study, not an independent replication. Post-block follow-up was only two weeks. This is preliminary evidence for enduring benefit.

**Practical implication, our inference.** A strategy’s usefulness depends on whether someone can sustain it alongside real responsibilities. Evaluate implementation burden as well as performance. Reducing access for a defined work period is an adaptation requiring its own test.

**Original Doom application, unvalidated.** Proposed doctrine: **“Protect the gate before judging the will.”** A Gate Audit records which phone functions the next project session actually needs, which interruptions can be postponed, and which essential contacts must remain reachable. Choose one reversible access change for a 30-minute session, define an output beforehand, and record completion, unplanned checks and any missed obligation. Repeat across comparable sessions before deciding whether to keep it.

This belongs inside W02-D02/W02-D04. It does not prescribe full internet blocking or promise a treatment effect. A later experiment could randomise comparable work sessions to normal access versus the chosen restriction, keeping task size and time budget similar. Report individual differences, compliance and artefact completion; a time-on-task impression alone is insufficient.

## Concrete pilot: Boundary Under Pressure

**Question.** Can participants use the card and demonstrate a considered boundary in a new situation without increasing the programme workload excessively?

**Design proposal, not yet run.** Use the charter's planning assumption of 6–8 consenting adult volunteers. Record the existing baseline scenario before teaching. Deliver the card within the existing day-three mission, with one paired rehearsal and an appropriate real-world attempt. Repeat with a new scenario on day 13 and at course completion. Use the existing 30-day follow-up for continuation evidence.

**Primary observable outcome.** Two reviewers independently classify the “Boundaries and commitments” response using the current categories: demonstrated, revise, not yet evidenced. Present responses in shuffled order with dates removed where feasible. Report how many participants move from each baseline category to each final category, and reviewer agreement before discussion. Do not assess accent, speed, volume, charisma or whether the other person approves.

**Secondary measures.**
- Whether the participant attempted the agreed conversation; distinguish a redacted contemporaneous record from later recollection.
- Whether an existing obligation was addressed, and whether the protected project session occurred.
- Minutes required, confusing instructions, unwanted consequences and withdrawals.
- At 30 days: one fresh example, whether the card is still used, and what support remains necessary.

**Provisional feasibility rule.** Continue development if at least three-quarters of enrolled participants complete the rehearsal and final scenario, median added burden stays within ten minutes in the existing mission, and no unresolved harmful consequence is attributed to the exercise. Report counts with denominators and all exceptions. These are product-design thresholds, not clinically meaningful cut-offs.

**Interpretation.** This small, uncontrolled pilot can expose unusable wording and measurement problems. It cannot establish efficacy, isolate the card from the rest of the course or prove lasting change. If feasible, pre-register a larger comparison of rehearsal plus feedback against matched lesson time; choose sample size from the intended outcome and a defensible smallest worthwhile difference, not an effect size borrowed from social-anxiety trials.
