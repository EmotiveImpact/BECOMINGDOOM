# Becoming Doom: seven-day evidence synthesis

**Coverage: 23–29 October 2026. Completed seven-report series.**

## Development decision

Build and test a small execution system first: a precise cue–action plan, observable output and a low-burden record of recovery and continuation. Next test whether a defined support component improves that behaviour. Preserve the six-week curriculum and its founder philosophy; the literature does not validate the complete programme or an indomitable personality.

This is a synthesis of **13 appraised sources**, not a new meta-analysis. Rankings combine methodological strength, relevance, testability and practical consequence. They are analyst development judgements, not numerical evidence scores. Effects across different populations and endpoints must not be compared as if they measured the same outcome. Some reviews may share primary studies; their sample sizes must not be added.

| **6** | **Campaign Review** | Broad but mainly associative adjustment evidence | Use as a reasoning exercise; assess decisions and subsequent actions rather than persistence alone |
| **7** | **Benchmark-to-Action** | Small comparison signal with very-low/low certainty; incremental value unresolved | Compare example-plus-feedback against identical feedback alone |
| **8** | **Earned Progress / Return-to-Work** | Indirect, preliminary feature and initiation evidence | Defer added interface mechanics; separate opening, returning, practising and producing work |

Maintenance measurement is a prerequisite across ranks, not another competing intervention. Drift Check, Denominator Check and the dossier can share one compact record. Rank 2 does not mean the Council should be removed; it means its incremental value and cost need evidence.

## Concrete first experiment: Command Trigger

**Question:** Can participants use one precise plan, and can its execution be measured without adding excessive work?

Use 6–8 consenting adults as a feasibility cohort, not an efficacy sample. Select one recurring project action per person and define an observable minimum output, eligible cue, response window and evidence criterion before allocation. Protect private material through redaction or demonstration.

Collect a seven-day baseline with neutral logging. Randomly assign introduction of the Command Trigger after day 7 or day 14. Continue through day 28, with checks at day 42 and 30 days later. Hold other guidance and reminders stable; record early card sharing, changed constraints and continued support.

**Primary outcome:** verified actions within the predeclared window divided by eligible opportunities, reported as exact individual counts. Cue occurrence is usually self-report; an artefact can corroborate action but cannot independently prove that the cue occurred. Record these separately.

**Secondary outcomes:** completed outputs, delay to resume after a missed opportunity, revisions to infeasible plans, reporting burden and withdrawals. Keep known noncompletion distinct from missing evidence; retain the enrolled denominator and show a conservative missing-as-incomplete bound.

**Feasibility gates:** at least 75% of expected records interpretable; median daily logging at most two minutes; participants can distinguish a cue from a vague intention; reviewers can consistently apply the completion criterion. These thresholds are original design choices, not evidence-derived cut-offs.

The staggered start helps inspect timing but remains vulnerable to maturation, learning and contamination. It cannot reliably estimate efficacy. If feasible, pre-register a larger active-comparator trial using a minimum worthwhile improvement and appropriate power calculation; do not borrow a dietary effect size or the tiny pilot's uplift.

## Sequence after the first pilot

1. **Calibrate measurement before expanding features.** Use the existing demonstrated / revise / not-yet-evidenced rubric. Test reviewer agreement and private evidence submission. Consolidate overlapping cards into one optional record.
2. **Test one continuation question.** Decide whether the question is support versus no additional support, Council versus individual review, or accountability added to the same support. These require different comparators. The 25 October delivery pilot and 28 October contract pilot must not be treated as interchangeable.
3. **Observe support withdrawal explicitly.** Timestamp when each reminder, meeting or reward stops. Continue the same neutral measurement schedule and record outside support. Thirty or ninety days is a limited window, not permanence.
4. **Evaluate boundaries and transfer.** Use a new scenario and an appropriate real-world attempt, not memorisation of the taught script. Measure how obligations were addressed and what action followed; another person's approval is not the criterion.
5. **Defer decoration until useful output is measurable.** If an emblem, comparison example or return prompt is tested, compare it with otherwise identical feedback. Do not bundle all new features and attribute any improvement to each one.

No pilot has been run. No recruitment, implementation or deployment is authorised by this research synthesis alone.

## Original doctrine and diagnostic opportunities

These are design proposals, not quotations from scientists or validated instruments.

- **“Command requires a trigger.”** Pair a chosen direction with an observable opportunity and feasible response. Diagnose missed cues and impractical actions descriptively.
- **“The Council earns its place after the meeting.”** Judge support by subsequent decisions, outputs and time cost, while recording social value separately.
- **“A boundary is a decision enacted under pressure.”** Assess behaviour in a changed situation, without grading charisma.
- **“Keep the aim answerable to evidence.”** Use Campaign Review to justify continuation, changed method or closure.
- **“Keep the denominator when enthusiasm fades.”** Include absent records and withdrawals when assessing continuation.

These refine existing doctrine without turning philosophy into empirical proof. Responsible self-interest, legitimate commitments and evidence-led adaptation remain the charter's definitions.

## Unresolved questions and research backlog

| Question | Why it matters | Next evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Do cue–action plans improve verified project output in the intended audience? | Domain transfer is the largest gap behind rank 1 | Active-comparator trial with observable outputs |
| Which support component adds enough value to justify time? | Meetings, peers, reminders and accountability are often bundled | Component comparison, cost and burden measures |
| What persists after support ends? | Longer treatment is often confused with independent maintenance | Explicit post-withdrawal follow-up and missing-data sensitivity |
| Does boundary rehearsal transfer beyond scenarios? | Existing evidence mainly measures symptoms | Real behavioural attempts, new scenarios and adverse consequences |
| When should persistence become adjustment? | Association does not supply a decision threshold | Prospective decisions, opportunity costs and subsequent outcomes |
| Are findings robust to exclusions and access gaps? | Some confidence labels and source appraisals remain unresolved | Assertiveness certainty/follow-up audit; gamification full text/version history; new maintenance image tables and clustering |

Keep the earlier unappraised flexibility-mindset and graded-task papers in the queue; their titles do not constitute evidence. Do not treat unresolved access as a negative result.
