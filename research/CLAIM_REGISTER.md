# Claim register

| Proposed statement | Classification | Support | Approved use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selfishness can be a virtue | Founder ethical position | R03 offers a related philosophical argument | Explain our definition and let participants reason about it |
| Earlier material should return throughout the course | Learning design informed by research | R04 | Use recall and spaced revisiting; exact schedule is provisional |
| If–then plans can help translate intentions into action | Research-informed method | R05 | Teach with context and no guaranteed effect |
| Autonomy supports motivation | Research-informed theory | R06 | Preserve choice, capability and relationships |
| Boundaries can be practised through increasingly challenging situations | Educational design | R08 plus our exercise design | Use proportionate practice and review |
| Mental models improve every decision | Unsupported universal claim | No source establishes this | Replace with relevance-based tool selection |
| Altruism destroys us | Unsupported universal claim | Not established | Discuss compulsive self-abandonment specifically |
| Failure is unacceptable | Ambiguous doctrine | No scientific claim | Reject unexamined repetition; permit learning and justified withdrawal |
| I become it because I said it | Identity declaration/metaphor | Founder language | Pair with chosen action and demonstrated capability |
| Becoming Doom transforms everyone in six weeks | Unvalidated product claim | Pilot not run | Do not publish |
| Promo makes listeners immediately sign up | Creative ambition | No campaign tested | Test understanding, relevance and actual interest |

Research supports selected teaching methods, not the completed programme's efficacy. Separate these levels in scripts, slide notes and sales copy.
