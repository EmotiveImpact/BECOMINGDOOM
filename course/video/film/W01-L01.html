09Three answers
A second case / Amara / ward nurse
THE SHIFTS THAT ATE HER STUDY.
Before
She wants the senior nurse role, which needs a course. Every week someone asks her to swap a shift, and she always says yes.
The move
She blocks Tuesday and Thursday evenings for study and tells the rota coordinator those two are fixed. Every other swap stays open.
After
Twelve weeks later the course is done. Her colleagues adjusted in a fortnight. One of them asked how she did it.
