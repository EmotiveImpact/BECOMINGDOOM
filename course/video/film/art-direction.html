BECOMING DOOM.03 / LIGHT AND COLOUR
Lighting

One source.

  • AKey: a single hard side light or rim light, high contrast, deep falloff.
  • BRed: one practical red source. A strip light, a phone, an exit sign, a reflection.
  • CTime: night only. No daylight, no dusk skies, no bright windows.
  • DFaces: lit enough to read the eyes. The rest of the body falls into shadow.
  • EThe arc: each week the face gets less light, until week 6 ends on a faceless hood.

BECOMING DOOM.06 / THE IMAGE RECIPE
GPT Image 2.5 / every image starts here

The image recipe.

[Subject and action], [setting], at night.

Low-key cinematic photograph. A single hard side light from [left or right] catches the face and hands; everything else falls into deep, pure black shadow. Around 70% of the frame is near black. One small practical red light source, [e.g. a thin red strip light], as the only colour. Black on black wardrobe and surfaces, matte and wet textures. Neutral, desaturated tones with no blue or teal cast, no warm beige. No daylight, no dusk sky, no bright windows. Shot on a 35mm or 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, high contrast, crushed blacks. Photorealistic, 16:9. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
  • 1Generate at 3840 by 2160.
  • 2Run the Doom grade on every image.
  • 3Check the eyes still read. If not, regenerate, don't brighten.
  • 4Keep space on the left or centre for words.
  • 5Cast rotates. Mostly men, men and women, diverse.
