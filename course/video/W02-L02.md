# W02-L02: Build conditions for execution

Video 5 of 18 • Week 2: Build the Fortress

- **Principles:** P13 Despise comfort and seek the friction
- **Model:** M02 Control and influence
- **Leads to:** workbook page W02-D02 (Day 9)
- **Evidence:** A control map plus one if-then plan
- **Protagonist:** East Asian man, late 20s
- **Recording format:** presenter to camera with full-screen slide cutaways. Each block is one slide. **Say** is the teleprompter script and sits in the slide's speaker notes.

---

## Slide 1 | title

**On screen:** BUILD THE CONDITIONS.
**Visual:** hood

**Say:**

You have priced your yeses. Now we make the work likely to happen.

Most people try to execute through willpower. They sit down in a noisy room with a phone beside them and call the result a lack of discipline. This lesson is about building the conditions instead.

## Slide 2 | question

**On screen:** How many times did your phone win the last session?

**Say:**

Think about your last work session. How many times did something else win? A notification. A message. A quick check that became twenty minutes.

Every one of those was a gate left open. Discipline is easier when the room is on your side.

## Slide 3 | red

**On screen:** WHAT CAN I DECIDE, INFLUENCE OR ONLY RESPOND TO?

**Say:**

The second thinking tool is control and influence. The question: what can I decide, what can I influence, and what can I only respond to?

Sort everything that touches your session into those three boxes. Then spend your effort where it actually moves something.

## Slide 4 | radial

**On screen:** Decide. Influence. Respond.

**Say:**

Here is a session and everything around it.

Some of it you decide outright. Where your phone is. When you start. Where you sit.

Some of it you can only influence. You can ask the people you live with for quiet, and you can agree a signal, but you cannot guarantee it.

And some of it you can only respond to. A late train. A sick child. A work emergency.

The limit on this tool: influence is partial. Do not pretend you control everything, and do not collapse into thinking you control nothing.

## Slide 5 | scene

**On screen:** CAN'T GUARANTEE A QUIET EVENING. CAN GUARANTEE A READY DESK.
**Visual:** scene

**Say:**

Jun shares a flat. He cannot guarantee an uninterrupted evening. His flatmates have their own lives.

What he can do is decide the parts that are his. His phone goes in a drawer. Notifications are off. The first task of the session is written on a card and propped against the monitor before he sits down.

## Slide 6 | compare

**On screen:** Three plans for the same evening

**Say:**

Three plans.

Hope. He sits down and relies on willpower. The first knock at the door ends the session.

Demand total control. He gets angry at every noise and spends the session managing other people.

The if-then plan. He predicts the most likely interruption and decides his response in advance. If the flat gets loud, then I put on headphones and run the twenty-five minute version.

The third plan accepts what he cannot control and prepares for it. That is the fortress.

## Slide 7 | grunge

**On screen:** COMFORT IS THE LEAK

**Say:**

Principle thirteen says despise comfort and seek the friction. Here it has a practical meaning. Put friction in front of distraction and remove it from in front of the work.

A phone in another room takes effort to check. A card with your first task removes the effort of starting.

## Slide 8 | steps

**On screen:** DRAW YOUR CONTROL MAP.

**Say:**

Pause the video when I tell you to.

One. List everything that has broken a session recently.

Two. Sort each one into decide, influence or respond.

Three. Choose one condition you decide and set it starting tonight.

Four. Write one if-then plan for the interruption that is most likely. Pause now and give it five minutes.

## Slide 9 | mission

**On screen:** Today's mission

**Say:**

Your mission is workbook page W02-D02, control the gates.

Build your control map and one if-then plan. Then use the plan in your next session and note whether it held.

## Slide 10 | close

**On screen:** DON'T RELY ON WILLPOWER. BUILD THE ROOM.
**Visual:** close

**Say:**

Next lesson we turn to the body: the capacity that every session runs on.

Don't rely on willpower. Build the room, then walk in and work.
