# Lesson film image prompts

Every image uses this base style, added to the end of each prompt:

> Cinematic photograph, 16:9, near-black background, one horizontal neon red light bar casting a hard stripe of red light, deep shadow, palette strictly red and black with natural skin tones, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

## The hooded figure arc

Each lesson opens on its protagonist in a black hoodie. The face sinks further into darkness week by week, until week 6 ends on a faceless figure whose hood holds only pitch black.

| Week | Hood portrait darkness |
|---|---|
| 1 | Face clearly visible, red bar across the eyes |
| 2 | Red bar across the eyes only, lower face in shadow |
| 3 | Only one eye and a cheekbone catch the red; most of the face in shadow |
| 4 | Face mostly darkness; a thin red rim on the jaw and a glint in the eyes |
| 5 | Face almost entirely black; faint red rim on the hood edge, barely visible eye glint |
| 6 | Inside the hood is a pitch black void, no face at all; red rim light on the hood only |

## Images per lesson

Four per lesson, saved as `<lesson>-hood.png`, `<lesson>-concept.png`, `<lesson>-scene.png`, `<lesson>-close.png`.

- **hood:** the protagonist in a black hoodie, three-quarter view, seated or standing, subject on the right third, darkness level for the week.
- **concept:** an object metaphor for the lesson, subject on the right half.
- **scene:** the worked example, subject on the right half, leaving the left 45% dark for type.
- **close:** the protagonist without a hood, facing camera, calm resolve, red light bar behind them, subject on the right third.
