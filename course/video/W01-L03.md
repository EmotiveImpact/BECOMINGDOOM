# W01-L03: Become nothing; choose something

Video 3 of 18 • Week 1: Claim the Throne

- **Principles:** P02 Accept that there is no inherent meaning; P06 Believe in your fucking self
- **Model:** none new
- **Leads to:** workbook page W01-D04 (Day 4)
- **Evidence:** The old label, the test action and the observed result
- **Protagonist:** Black woman, late 20s, natural hair tied back
- **Recording format:** presenter to camera with full-screen slide cutaways. Each block is one slide. **Say** is the teleprompter script and sits in the slide's speaker notes.

---

## Slide 1 | title

**On screen:** BECOME NOTHING. CHOOSE SOMETHING.
**Visual:** hood

**Say:**

You have a doctrine and you have said your first no. Now we deal with the voice that says you are not the kind of person who does this.

Lesson three: become nothing, choose something. We are going to take one label you carry about yourself and put it on trial.

## Slide 2 | hook

**On screen:** Finish this sentence honestly. I AM NOT THE KIND OF PERSON WHO...
**Visual:** concept

**Say:**

Finish this sentence. I am not the kind of person who...

Who sells. Who speaks in public. Who finishes things. Who is good with money. Who writes. Most people can finish it in under a second, which tells you how often they have said it.

That sentence sounds like a description. In practice it works like a verdict. It decides what you will try before you have tried it.

## Slide 3 | radial

**On screen:** One life. Many labels. One next action.

**Say:**

Look at how labels collect. Not a business person. Bad with numbers. Not creative. Too old. Each one began as a summary of a few moments, often moments from years ago, and then it hardened.

Principle two says: accept that there is no inherent meaning. Applied here, it means no label was written into you. You can hold it up and inspect it.

Becoming nothing means releasing the label. Your history, your dignity and your responsibilities all stay. What goes is the verdict.

## Slide 4 | red

**On screen:** TURN THE VERDICT INTO AN EXPERIMENT.

**Say:**

Here is the move. You turn the verdict into an experiment.

A verdict says what you are. An experiment asks what happens when you do one small thing. A verdict cannot be tested. An experiment can, and it produces evidence by the end of the week.

## Slide 5 | scene

**On screen:** I AM NOT A BUSINESS PERSON. ONE CONVERSATION THIS WEEK.
**Visual:** scene

**Say:**

Meet Ama. She is a skilled bookkeeper and she wants her own clients. Every time she thinks about it, the same sentence arrives. I am not a business person.

So she runs an experiment. She asks one small business owner for twenty minutes to talk about the problem they have with their books. No pitch. Just questions.

## Slide 6 | compare

**On screen:** Three ways to handle the label

**Say:**

There are three ways to handle a label.

Obey it. She does nothing, and the label is confirmed by default. Not by evidence. By absence.

Hype over it. She tells herself she is a CEO now and posts about it. It feels strong for an evening. It produces nothing anyone can check.

Test it. She has one real conversation and writes down what happened. Principle six says believe in your fucking self. In this course belief is a decision to act, and the act is what we measure.

Ama learns that the owner hates their current system and would pay to stop thinking about it. That is information. The label has nothing to say about it.

## Slide 7 | stats

**On screen:** Small enough to do. Real enough to count.

**Say:**

Keep the experiment small. One conversation. Twenty minutes. No pitch.

Small enough that you will actually do it this week. Real enough that the answer counts.

## Slide 8 | steps

**On screen:** PUT ONE LABEL ON TRIAL.

**Say:**

Pause the video when I tell you to.

One. Write the label exactly as you say it to yourself. Use your own words.

Two. Name one small action that the label says you cannot do.

Three. Shrink that action until it fits into this week. If it needs a website, it is too big. If it needs one message, it is about right.

Four. Decide in advance what result would count as evidence. Then pause and write it down.

## Slide 9 | mission

**On screen:** Today's mission

**Say:**

Your mission is workbook page W01-D04, release the label.

Name one label. Take one small action that tests it. Record what actually happened.

You are not required to feel confident. You are required to collect evidence.

## Slide 10 | close

**On screen:** YOU ARE NOT A VERDICT. YOU ARE A WORK IN PROGRESS.
**Visual:** close

**Say:**

Tomorrow you will choose the project that carries you to Doomsday. The label you test today is often the one that would have stopped you choosing it.

You are not a verdict. You are a work in progress. Choose something, then test it.

## Production notes

- Delivery: warm and steady. The hype option should be played straight, never mocked.
- The stats slide uses numbers from the exercise itself. Make no claims about outcomes.
- Ethics: the interview is a conversation, not a sales call. Participants must not misrepresent why they are asking.
