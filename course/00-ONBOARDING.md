# Before Day 1: Enter the Throne Room

Becoming Doom • participant onboarding • complete this before the first session (about 40 minutes).

## The proposition

Your life is a legitimate object of your own ambition. For six weeks you will invest in it deliberately, give that investment your best work, and let reality judge the work.

We call the organising philosophy **selfishness as a virtue**: deliberate responsibility for your own flourishing. It is demanding. It asks you to choose what your future is worth, protect the resources it needs, and become capable of delivering what you claim matters.

The capacity we are building is **indomitable will**: purposeful persistence, joined to the judgement to change your method when reality tells you to.

## How the six weeks are built

| Week | Stage | The question | What you will prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claim the Throne | Whose life am I building? | A personal doctrine, a first considered no and a Doom Project charter |
| 2 | Build the Fortress | Can I trust my own execution? | A tested command sheet, protected work and a recovery record |
| 3 | Enter the Laboratory | What can I actually do? | A demonstrated skill, a first version and a checked Doombot |
| 4 | Command the Room | Can I hold my ground with people? | A real conversation, prepared and honestly debriefed |
| 5 | Build the Empire | Will anyone use this? | A live offer or release, real responses and a revision |
| 6 | Become Dangerous | Does it hold together under change? | Doomsday demonstration, evidence dossier and a 90-day plan |

Four territories run through every week: **Command** (your decisions), **Body** (your capacity), **Capability** (what you can do) and **Empire** (what you build for others). The method is always the same: **Decide, Organise, Outwork, Maintain.**

Nothing is left behind. Week one's boundaries return in week four under heavier pressure. Week two's tools return in week six without a prompt. Each week assumes you still own the last one.

## Your weekly rhythm (about 6 hours 10 minutes)

- **Monday to Friday, 50 minutes a day:** 5 minutes of recall without notes, up to 10 minutes of teaching, 30 minutes of action on your Doom Project, 5 minutes on the command record.
- **Saturday, 75 minutes:** the Council of Doom.
- **Sunday, 45 minutes:** the Reckoning, a weekly review and preparation.

Project work *is* the mission. There is no second pile of homework. If you have less time than this, shrink the project scope with your facilitator rather than skipping days. Physical training sits outside these minutes and is adapted to you.

## The Doom Project

You will choose one project in week one and carry it to Doomsday. It must be:

- **useful to a named audience** other than you alone;
- **finishable in a demonstrable first version** within six weeks of your available time;
- **yours to decide**, so you do not need anyone's permission to start it.

Examples: a portfolio for the clients you actually want; a short paid workshop pilot; a tool that saves your team an hour a week; a first chapter, released to ten readers; a service offer tested with five prospects. Revenue is welcome but optional.

## The daily command record

Every day ends with five lines. Keep it under five minutes.

1. **Priority:** what was I protecting today?
2. **Pressure:** what pulled against it?
3. **Response:** what did I actually do?
4. **Evidence:** what exists now that did not exist this morning?
5. **Correction:** what will I do differently tomorrow?

From week two add one more line: **Model:** which tool helped, or why none was needed?

## The reset: when you miss a day

Notice. Record. Repair. Resume.

A missed session is information. Write what happened, separate what you controlled from what you did not, choose the next feasible step, and take it within a day where you can. No all-night catch-ups, no punishment, no pretending it did not happen. Genuine emergencies earn more time. Never invent a lapse to complete an exercise.

## The words we use, and what they mean here

- **Self-interest** weighs long-term consequences, relationships, commitments and fair exchange. Obeying every impulse is its opposite.
- **Courage to be disliked** means tolerating possible disapproval while making a considered choice. You never need to seek rejection.
- **Ruthless** describes your focus and your cuts. It never describes how you treat people.
- **Killer instinct** means acting decisively while an opportunity is real.
- **Become nothing** means releasing limiting labels. Your dignity, history and responsibilities stay.
- **Exceptional** means unusually serious preparation, execution, feedback and correction. It says nothing about your worth relative to anyone else.
- **A setback** is information. Persistence answers to evidence and to sensible exit criteria.

## The fourteen principles

These are the creed of Becoming Doom. You will meet each one properly in the week it is taught.

1. Put yourself above all people.
2. Accept that there is no inherent meaning.
3. Develop your killer instinct.
4. Hold a vision and be ruthless.
5. Build your empire.
6. Believe in your fucking self.
7. Vow to become a master.
8. Burn the bridge of permission.
9. Radical accountability is absolute power.
10. Manipulate chaos into currency.
11. Master the art of the tactical withdrawal.
12. Treat your body as the ultimate weapon.
13. Despise comfort and seek the friction.
14. Embrace your finite nature.

You are not required to adopt a worldview. You can hold religious convictions, love your family fiercely and collaborate generously while demonstrating every standard in this course. We assess what you do.

## How you will be assessed

Six dimensions, each rated **demonstrated**, **revise** or **not yet evidenced**:

1. Agency and self-interest
2. Boundaries and commitments
3. Judgement
4. Execution and recovery
5. Capability and value
6. Adaptation

You pass on demonstrated judgement and action. No pass depends on someone else saying yes, on income, on charisma or on a perfect streak. Every "revise" comes with a specific request and you may resubmit. Private evidence can be redacted or shown privately. You will never be asked to record another person.

## What this course will not promise you

Six weeks of structured practice and real evidence of progress. That is the offer. Becoming Doom has not yet been through a full pilot, so we make no claim of guaranteed income, invulnerability or permanent transformation. Identity declarations choose a direction; action and evidence build capability. This is education, not therapy or medical advice.
