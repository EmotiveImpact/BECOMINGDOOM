# Becoming Doom: course audit and slide decks

6 November 2026

## The short answer

The course is strong and ready. All 18 v2 lessons follow the same seven-part shape, each one teaches a real skill through a named person in a believable situation, and each ends with something the viewer does that day. Every one of the 14 principles (P01 to P14) and all 10 thinking tools (M01 to M10) is taught at least once. Day and workbook references match the 42-day plan, the cast is varied and leans male as asked, and the scripts are clean on British spelling with no em dashes.

Because content was locked on 31 October, I only changed what was clearly broken or missing. Six fixes are listed below. Three bigger ideas are left for you to decide.

## For you to decide

1. **Lesson 1 could add a "cost of staying the same" beat.** One slide showing what another year of the current pattern costs would raise the stakes before the first tool.
2. **The middle of every lesson repeats the same pattern** ("three ways", "where people go wrong", "three things to keep"). It is easy to follow, but across 18 lessons it may feel templated. We could vary it in some weeks.
3. **Two identity principles appear only once.** P02 and P06 are each taught in a single lesson. They could come back briefly in the final lesson (W06-L03) so the course closes on who the viewer has become.

A small note: Daniel's protected evening is Thursday in lesson 1 and Wednesday in lesson 2. This is harmless, but easy to align if you prefer.

## The slide decks

All 18 decks are built to the locked look: Inter Tight only, red and black, red full stops, no slice or glitch effects, content centred top to bottom, and every second slide a full-screen scene image with text over it. Speaker notes carry the script for each slide.

Lesson 1 has its real images in place. In the other 17 decks the scene slides have dark image frames, each carrying a written description of the shot it needs, ready for the image stage. The logo is a placeholder until the new logo direction is chosen.

The decks are private to you until you share them from each deck's Share menu.
