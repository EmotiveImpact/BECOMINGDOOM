# Becoming Doom: reading companion

Eighteen chapters, one for each video lesson, plus a short card for every book on the reading list. Written 31 October 2026.

Each chapter goes deeper into the books behind its lesson: who wrote them, what they argue, how the course uses the ideas, where the tool stops working, two worked examples and the common mistakes. Everything is in our own words. The ideas are credited to their authors and no text from the books is reproduced, beyond a couple of very short, famous attributed phrases.

## Before publishing

None of the books has been read in full by the team. The chapters describe each book's main ideas in general terms, as they are widely understood. Before the companion goes to participants, someone should check each chapter against a lawful copy of the book it discusses, and the four health, money and research cautions should stay in.
