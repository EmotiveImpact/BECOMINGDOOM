# Becoming Doom: the book list

Compiled 31 October 2026. Two parts. Part 1 is what the course is actually built on today, taken from the repo's research register (`research/sources/R01` to `R10`). Part 2 is a reading list of books that match each principle, tool and lesson. The lessons were written in our own words and do not quote these books.

## Part 1: what the course currently draws on

Only three of the ten registered sources are books, and none has been read in full. The team read publisher or author overviews, or a public excerpt. The register says to get lawful copies before making any claim about a specific chapter.

| ID | Source | Type | How much was read | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Natalie Lue, *The Joy of Saying No* (2022) | Book | Author overview only | W01-L02, boundary work |
| R02 | Ichiro Kishimi and Fumitake Koga, *The Courage to Be Disliked* | Book | Publisher overview only | W01-L02 (it gives the lesson its title), W04-L03 |
| R03 | Ayn Rand, *The Virtue of Selfishness* (1964) | Philosophy book | Public excerpt (Ayn Rand Lexicon) | W01-L01, the whole "selfishness as a virtue" frame |

| R06 | Self-Determination Theory (Deci and Ryan) | Theory overview | Overview read | Course design: choice, competence, the Council |
| R07 | Farnam Street, mental models collection | Website | Public page inspected | The ten thinking tools |

Background only, kept out of participant reading on purpose: Chin-Ning Chu, *Thick Face, Black Heart* (1992).

The emblem: Doctor Doom first appeared in Marvel's *Fantastic Four* comics (Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, 1962). The course borrows his traits only and uses no artwork, footage or dialogue.
