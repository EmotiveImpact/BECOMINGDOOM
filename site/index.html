The First No

Think about the last time you said yes while quietly hoping they would withdraw the request. This lesson starts there.

In about eight minutes you will separate a real obligation from approval pressure, write one or two sentences you can actually say, and rehearse the follow-up question that usually makes you cave.

  • The written lesson, ready to read straight away
  • The one-page workbook you can fill in on screen or print
  • A field mission for today, small enough to do and real enough to matter

It is lesson two from week one of Becoming Doom, unedited, so you see exactly how the course teaches.

The field mission

Use it once, record what you predicted and what happened, and spend the time you protected.

Someone else's request opens a decision. It does not settle it.

Who it is for

People who can describe an impressive future and keep spending today protecting other people's impression of them. If your own project keeps losing to favours, extra revisions and evenings you never meant to give away, start here.

Who it is not for: anyone looking for permission to be cruel. The lesson teaches honouring real commitments and saying a calm, clear no to the rest. Hostility earns no credit.
