Privacy

Privacy notice

What we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

  • Free lesson sign-up: your first name and email address.
  • Applications: your name, email, optional contact handle and your answers to the application questions.
  • Workbook answers: stored only in your own browser on your device. They are never sent to us.

This site uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we use it

We use your email to send the lesson workbook and a short series of emails about Becoming Doom, because you asked for them. We use application answers to decide whether the course suits you and to contact you about your place. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes it

Email addresses are held by our email provider and applications by our form provider. Payments, if you join, are handled by our checkout provider; we never see your card details. These providers process data on our behalf and only for these purposes. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Applications from people who do not join are deleted twelve months after the cohort starts.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or unsubscribe using the link in any email. To make a request, reply to any of our emails or write to us directly. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
