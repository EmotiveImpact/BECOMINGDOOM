# Week 4: Command the Room

**Question:** Can I pursue my interests in contact with other people?

New creed emphasis: P03, P11. New models: M05, M06. Source cards: R01, R02, R08.

## The change to look for

From avoiding a consequential conversation to preparing, conducting and learning from it.

## Three core lesson briefs

### W04-L01: Read interests without mind-reading

Teach: Introduce incentives. State what you know, what you infer and what you should ask.

Worked example: A client may value speed more than a discount; ask rather than assume the motive.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

### W04-L02: Hold the line; consider what follows

Teach: Introduce second-order consequences. Prepare a clear request, alternatives and limits.

Worked example: An unnecessary discount may win today’s sale while creating an expectation you cannot sustain.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

### W04-L03: Choose the engagement

Teach: Rehearse criticism, negotiation and tactical withdrawal. Use a calm direct response and an explicit decision rule.

Worked example: Decline poor terms with an alternative, or withdraw without turning the conversation into a contest.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

## Seven-day delivery map

### Day 22: Map the interests

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D01.

Recall: Recall the first no and apply it to a stronger demand.

Mission: Use M05 to prepare a real conversation. Identify unanswered questions about the other party.

Evidence: Interest map separating evidence and inference.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 23: Think beyond the answer

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D02.

Recall: Reuse opportunity cost rather than inventing a new framework.

Mission: Use M06 to compare accepting, negotiating and declining a proposal.

Evidence: Consequences sheet including a future cost.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 24: Make the request

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D03.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 25: Hold a real conversation

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D04.

Recall: Select an earlier model without a facilitator prompt.

Mission: Conduct an appropriate pitch, boundary conversation or negotiation.

Evidence: Privacy-conscious factual account of what was said and agreed.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 26: Debrief the response

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D05.

Recall: Revisit self-belief without ignoring valid criticism.

Mission: Separate rejection, actionable criticism and uncertainty. Decide whether to persist, change or withdraw.

Evidence: Debrief and a proportionate follow-up action.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 27: Council: hold your frame

Time: 75 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D06.

Recall: Combine a boundary, M05 and M06.

Mission: Role-play a reasonable challenge with agreed limits. Explain your decision.

Evidence: Feedback and improved response.

### Day 28: Choose your next engagement

Time: 45 minutes. Workbook ID: W04-D07.

Recall: Recall all six available models and select one relevant pair.

Mission: Review conversations, promises and project progress.

Evidence: Next engagement plan with continue/change/withdraw criteria.

## Workbook and slide production

Use one daily page per workbook ID, plus a weekly evidence review. Each page needs the mission, response fields, an original worked example and the evidence criterion. Slide production should follow the three lesson briefs; do not turn every workbook instruction into a slide.

## Weekly review

Use the common assessment rubric. Identify missing evidence and a feasible revision; no pass depends on a positive response from another person. Before the next week, give one specific observation, one correction and one next action.
