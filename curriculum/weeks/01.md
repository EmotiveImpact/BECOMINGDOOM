# Week 1: Claim the Throne

**Question:** Whose life am I actually building?

New creed emphasis: P01, P02, P04, P06, P08. New models: none; establish the foundation. Source cards: R01, R02, R03, R08.

## The change to look for

From automatic agreement to one considered choice in service of a chosen priority.

## Three core lesson briefs

### W01-L01: Selfishness as a virtue

Teach: Distinguish chosen responsibility from compulsive approval-seeking. Map where time and attention serve borrowed priorities.

Worked example: A freelancer accepts another unpaid revision while postponing their own portfolio. Compare an automatic yes, an honest no and a renegotiated scope.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

### W01-L02: The courage to be disliked

Teach: Separate another person’s reaction from your decision and responsibilities. Practise a clear refusal without a defensive biography.

Worked example: A friend asks for a favour during a protected session. State availability without blaming the friend.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

### W01-L03: Become nothing; choose something

Teach: Question a limiting label and select a finite project. Replace an identity verdict with a testable next action.

Worked example: “I am not a business person” becomes an experiment: interview one potential client about a real problem.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

## Seven-day delivery map

### Day 1: The borrowed life

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D01.

Recall: What did I choose, and what did I assume I had to do?

Mission: Record the baseline scenario before teaching. Audit yesterday’s time, attention and promises.

Evidence: Baseline response plus three approval-driven choices.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 2: Your interests count

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D02.

Recall: Explain self-interest without confusing it with impulse.

Mission: Write a one-page doctrine: what matters, what it costs, responsibilities and one protected priority.

Evidence: Doctrine v1 with a specific resource allocation.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 3: The first no

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D03.

Recall: Recall yesterday’s protected priority without notes.

Mission: Rehearse a proportionate refusal, then use it on an appropriate low-stakes request.

Evidence: Boundary record: words, predicted reaction, actual reaction and reflection.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 4: Release the label

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D04.

Recall: Recall the first no and what the response actually showed.

Mission: Choose a limiting identity claim and take one small action that tests it.

Evidence: Old label, test action and observed result.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 5: Choose the Doom Project

Time: 50 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D05.

Recall: Which automatic yes could threaten this mission?

Mission: Choose audience, useful deliverable, available time and a six-week success criterion. Take the first step.

Evidence: One-page project charter plus first action.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 6: Council: claim your mission

Time: 75 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D06.

Recall: Explain the difference between disapproval and useful criticism.

Mission: Present the doctrine and project; practise responding to one reasonable objection.

Evidence: Feedback, one revision and next commitment.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

### Day 7: The first reckoning

Time: 45 minutes. Workbook ID: W01-D07.

Recall: Recall the doctrine and one example of choosing freely.

Mission: Review week-one evidence. Choose what to stop, keep and protect next week.

Evidence: Weekly review and next week’s protected sessions.

Carry forward: relate today’s choice to the personal doctrine and a boundary or considered commitment. Log priority, pressure, response, evidence and correction.

## Workbook and slide production

Use one daily page per workbook ID, plus a weekly evidence review. Each page needs the mission, response fields, an original worked example and the evidence criterion. Slide production should follow the three lesson briefs; do not turn every workbook instruction into a slide.

## Weekly review

Use the common assessment rubric. Identify missing evidence and a feasible revision; no pass depends on a positive response from another person. Before the next week, give one specific observation, one correction and one next action.
