# Week 6: Become Dangerous

**Question:** Can I retain judgement when circumstances change?

New creed emphasis: P14. New models: M09, M10. Source cards: R04, R06, R09.

## The change to look for

From following prompts to independently choosing a response and showing evidence.

## Three core lesson briefs

### W06-L01: Think in uncertainty

Teach: Introduce probabilistic thinking. State plausible outcomes, confidence and what would change your view.

Worked example: One enthusiastic user is evidence of interest, not proof of a dependable market.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

### W06-L02: Preserve room to recover

Teach: Introduce margin of safety. Keep proportionate time or resource buffers and contingency options.

Worked example: Reserve a local demo copy and a smaller demonstration if a tool is unavailable.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

### W06-L03: A finite life; a chosen next chapter

Teach: Assemble proof, reflect on trade-offs and allocate the next ninety days.

Worked example: Choose a sustainable next milestone and state which attractive alternatives will remain undone.

Production: 8–10 minute lesson; one proposition, one mistaken response, one improved response, one guided rehearsal. Use the matching daily mission below for application. Avoid adding a second assignment.

Mission: Use M09 to map remaining unknowns and choose one useful check.

## Workbook and slide production

Use one daily page per workbook ID, plus a weekly evidence review. Each page needs the mission, response fields, an original worked example and the evidence criterion. Slide production should follow the three lesson briefs; do not turn every workbook instruction into a slide.

## Weekly review

Use the common assessment rubric. Identify missing evidence and a feasible revision; no pass depends on a positive response from another person. Before the next week, give one specific observation, one correction and one next action.
