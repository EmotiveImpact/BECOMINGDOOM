# Course architecture

## One spine, four territories

Self-interest gives the mission legitimacy. Courage to be disliked protects it from approval pressure. Clear thinking improves choices. Execution and physical readiness sustain action. Capability and leverage give ambition practical effect. Indomitable will integrates these capacities under changing conditions.

Command governs decisions in every week. Body supports energy, attention and recovery in every week, with the main setup in week two. Capability becomes explicit in week three. Empire connects capability to useful work and exchange in week five. Each territory remains active after introduction.

| Week | Environment and question | New emphasis | Weekly proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Throne: whose life am I building? | Self-interest, permission, labels, boundaries | Doctrine, baseline, first considered no, project charter |
| 2 | Fortress: can I trust my execution? | Attention, body, commitments, opportunity cost | Tested command sheet, protected work, recovery record |
| 3 | Laboratory: what can I now do? | Investigation, skill practice, prototype, automation | Demonstrated skill, prototype and checked repeatable aid |
| 4 | Room: can I hold my ground? | Requests, negotiation, criticism, withdrawal | Real conversation, preparation and accurate debrief |
| 5 | Empire: will anyone use this? | Value, offer, leverage, feedback | Live offer or release, real responses and revision |
| 6 | Doomsday: does it hold together? | Uncertainty, contingencies, independent judgement | Demonstration, bounded pivot, dossier and 90-day plan |

## Workload: proposed 370 minutes each week

Monday–Friday: 50 minutes daily, including a five-minute recall/check-in, up to ten minutes of new teaching, thirty minutes of action/project work and five minutes of review. Three main lessons occupy Monday–Wednesday; Thursday and Friday largely apply existing learning. Saturday Council: 75 minutes. Sunday review and preparation: 45 minutes. Total: 6 hours 10 minutes. The daily log is included, not extra. Personal physical activity is adapted to circumstances and sits outside this taught-work estimate; no universal training prescription.

Project work is the mission, not a second workload. A participant with less time reduces project scope with the facilitator. Optional reading is enrichment, never a prerequisite for passing.

## Repeatable lesson pattern

Doom Has → Principle → Reality → Build → Practice → Mastery.

Use the fictional inspiration as an opening lens, then move to an ordinary real decision. Explain one proposition, show a worked example including a mistake, guide a rehearsal, assign a mission and state what counts as evidence. Mastery is the direction of travel, not a six-week claim.

## How compounding actually happens

1. Recall without notes: explain an older principle and a recent application.
2. Revisit it within the current project or constraint.
3. Add at most two reasoning tools in the week, with examples of when each is unhelpful.
4. Ask learners to select and justify a tool rather than name-drop every tool.
5. Return feedback and allow revision.
6. Fade assistance: scripts in week one, checklists in weeks two and three, learner-selected methods in weeks four and five, independent selection in week six.

For every day after introduction, week-one self-interest and boundaries remain available. A no is not required every day: a considered yes, renegotiated agreement or honoured commitment can demonstrate agency.

Research sources R04 and R05 support retrieval/distributed practice and if–then planning respectively. The particular six-week arrangement, thresholds and pressure progression are our design hypotheses, to evaluate in the pilot.

## Daily command record

Priority → Pressure → Response → Evidence → Correction.

After week two add one short field: which available model helped, or why no model was needed? Do not force complex analysis for trivial decisions. Keep the log within five minutes.

## Council of Doom: 75 minutes

5 minutes: arrival and recall. 10: selected lesson from founder's own mission. 30: rotating demonstrations and feedback. 15: scenario rehearsal. 10: corrections and next commitments. 5: closing. All learners submit weekly; speaking slots rotate. Use private facilitator review where evidence is sensitive. The final Doomsday Council has its own running order in ASSESSMENT.md.

## Recovery and assessment

The reset is notice, record, repair, resume. Aim to identify a feasible next step within a day; genuine emergencies justify more time. Never fabricate a lapse to satisfy the assignment. Use a disruption rehearsal if none occurs. Completion rests on demonstrated judgement and action, not flawless streaks, charisma or income.
