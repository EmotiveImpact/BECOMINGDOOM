# The fourteen principles

Headings are preserved from the supplied conversation. Interpretations below are this edition’s proposed teaching specification. Read alongside PROJECT_CHARTER.md.

## P01: Put yourself above all people

Main week: 1.

Meaning: Prioritise your flourishing while considering chosen responsibilities and consequences.

Behavioural evidence: Name a priority, protect its resources and explain a trade-off.

Misinterpretation to address: Treating others as disposable or escaping a commitment without addressing it.

## P02: Accept that there is no inherent meaning

Main week: 1.

Meaning: Explore chosen meaning as a philosophical position; participants may retain religious or other beliefs.

Behavioural evidence: Write a personally endorsed mission and reasons for pursuing it.

Misinterpretation to address: Teaching nihilism as a proven fact or demanding a worldview conversion.

## P03: Develop your killer instinct

Main week: 4.

Meaning: Act decisively on a considered opportunity and tolerate rejection.

Behavioural evidence: Make a direct request within a defined time window.

Misinterpretation to address: Aggression, intimidation or careless action.

## P04: Hold a vision and be ruthless

Main week: 1.

Meaning: Be exacting about focus, trade-offs and unnecessary work.

Behavioural evidence: Cut a low-value task and protect a mission milestone.

Misinterpretation to address: Refusing new evidence or neglecting essential responsibilities.

## P05: Build your empire

Main week: 5.

Meaning: Create useful assets, systems and fair exchanges.

Behavioural evidence: Test a project with intended users and document repeatable delivery.

Misinterpretation to address: Equating worth with wealth or promising guaranteed revenue.

## P06: Believe in your fucking self

Main week: 1.

Meaning: Trust your capacity to take a next step and learn.

Behavioural evidence: Take an uncertain action, seek feedback and revise.

Misinterpretation to address: Confusing confidence with factual correctness.

## P07: Vow to become a master

Main week: 3.

Meaning: Commit to sustained, feedback-informed skill development.

Behavioural evidence: Demonstrate a subskill and a measured improvement.

Misinterpretation to address: Claiming mastery after six weeks.

## P08: Burn the bridge of permission

Main week: 1.

Meaning: Release unnecessary approval-seeking; identify real dependencies.

Behavioural evidence: Act without asking for reassurance when authorisation is genuinely yours.

Misinterpretation to address: Ignoring consent, professional obligations or permissions that are actually required.

## P09: Radical accountability is absolute power

Main week: 2.

Meaning: Own controllable decisions and responses without inventing control over everything.

Behavioural evidence: Separate own action, external constraint and next response in a debrief.

Misinterpretation to address: Blaming people for illness, disadvantage or other people’s conduct.

## P10: Manipulate chaos into currency

Main week: 5.

Meaning: Find useful responses and opportunities within disruption.

Behavioural evidence: Adapt a project to a changed constraint and explain the value created.

Misinterpretation to address: Exploiting distress or assuming all losses can be monetised.

## P11: Master the art of the tactical withdrawal

Main week: 4.

Meaning: Leave a poor engagement deliberately and preserve resources.

Behavioural evidence: Set exit criteria and justify continue, change or withdraw.

Misinterpretation to address: Calling every uncomfortable task a bad engagement.

## P12: Treat your body as the ultimate weapon

Main week: 2.

Meaning: Support attention and action through appropriate movement and recovery.

Behavioural evidence: Maintain a personally suitable readiness practice and adjust it sensibly.

Misinterpretation to address: Sleep deprivation, punishment, exclusion by physical ability or unsafe challenges.

## P13: Despise comfort and seek the friction

Main week: 2.

Meaning: Practise purposeful discomfort that serves a chosen goal.

Behavioural evidence: Complete a proportionate challenge and explain what was learned.

Misinterpretation to address: Treating rest as weakness or suffering as evidence of value.

## P14: Embrace your finite nature

Main week: 6.

Meaning: Use finite time to choose priorities and commitments.

Behavioural evidence: Allocate the next ninety days and state what will be left undone.

Misinterpretation to address: Urgency theatre or a facilitator forcing personal disclosures.

## Doctrine hierarchy

Creed = beliefs. Laws = behaviour. Standards = minimums. Missions = action. Rituals = repetition. Tests = proof.

Proposed laws: choose deliberately; protect the chosen priority; distinguish facts from interpretations; honour or renegotiate commitments; submit to useful feedback; repair and resume. These are new working formulations, not recovered historical text.

Standards: truthful evidence, proportionate practice, constructive feedback, clear scope and active correction. The founder undertakes comparable missions and shares selected evidence.
