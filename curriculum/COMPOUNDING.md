## Retrieval schedule

Every weekday starts with a five-minute recall/check-in. New tools return the following day, at the Council, in the Sunday review and in a later project decision. Exact retrieval questions live in each daily brief. Weekly reviews include one early principle, one previous tool and one current application. Participants answer before reopening notes, then correct their answer.

## Scaffold removal

Week 1: scripts, explicit examples, low stakes. Week 2: planning templates. Week 3: guided model selection and feedback. Week 4: learner chooses an older model for a real conversation. Week 5: learner defends the model and measurement choice. Week 6: facilitator supplies the changed situation, not the method.

## Crescendo

Baseline: before instruction, record a response to approval pressure, a deadline and a personal ambition. Final: use a comparable scenario with changed surface details and the same scoring dimensions. Combine this comparison with real-world outputs. Self-report alone does not establish transformation; improved scenario performance alone does not prove durable change. A day-30 continuation check tests whether practices have persisted.
