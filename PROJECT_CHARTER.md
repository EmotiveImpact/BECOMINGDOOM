# Project charter

## The transformation

Become the author of your life and develop the judgement, capability and sustained action required to pursue it. The organising philosophy is **selfishness as a virtue**: deliberate responsibility for your own flourishing. The central capacity is **indomitable will**: purposeful persistence with the ability to update methods and decisions when reality changes.

The participant should finish able to protect a chosen priority, tolerate disapproval, reason through a consequential decision, demonstrate a useful capability, expose a real project to feedback and recover intelligently from a setback.

## Audience and tension

Initial audience hypothesis: ambitious adults whose effort is fragmented by approval-seeking, overcommitment, abandoned projects and borrowed expectations. They want greater agency and something concrete to show for it. Validate this hypothesis in pilot interviews before choosing the final sales audience.

The underlying tension: they can describe an impressive future but repeatedly spend today's resources protecting other people's impression of them. We must also recognise genuine responsibilities, limited resources and constraints outside personal control.

## Settled founder direction

- Programme: Becoming Doom. Brand direction: Become Dangerous.
- Duration: six weeks, 42 days. Earlier eight-week and ninety-day formats are superseded as the taught duration; retain ninety days as continuation.
- Territories: Command, Body, Capability, Empire.
- Method: Decide, Organise, Outwork, Maintain.
- One Doom Project, weekly Council of Doom, final Doomsday demonstration.
- All fourteen supplied creed headings retained.
- Self-interest, boundaries, courage to be disliked and freedom from people-pleasing begin in week one and remain active throughout.
- Two reasoning tools added weekly from week two; previous tools are reused, not replaced.
- Each week's learning compounds towards increasingly independent action.
- Thick Face, Black Heart is not a named participant-facing framework in this first iteration.
- Matte black, cinematic and commanding creative direction, with readable teaching materials.
- Written lessons, workbook system and slide production must follow a single curriculum map.

## Definitions we teach explicitly

Self-interest considers long-term consequences, relationships, commitments and exchange. It does not mean obeying every impulse. Courage to be disliked means tolerating possible disapproval while making considered choices; it does not mean seeking rejection. Become nothing means releasing limiting labels, not erasing dignity, history or responsibilities. Exceptionalism means unusually serious preparation, execution, feedback and correction, not superiority of human worth. Aggressive pursuit means energetic, persistent, purposeful action. A setback is information; persistence is subject to evidence and sensible exit criteria.

## Course promise and limits

Working promise: **Stop asking permission to build a life you can stand behind. Develop the will, judgement and capability to act.**

A six-week course offers structured practice and demonstrable progress. We have not yet run a pilot and cannot claim validated transformation, guaranteed income, invulnerability or universal results. Identity declarations select a direction; action and evidence establish capability.

## Evidence of completion

Personal doctrine; tested daily operating system; boundary and decision records; demonstrated capability; one useful automation or equivalent repeatable aid; real consequential conversation; Doom Project tested with its intended audience; evidence dossier; ninety-day plan.

## Pending commercial decisions

Price, cohort size, launch date, delivery platform, facilitator availability and audience niche remain open. Use a pilot cohort of 6–8 adults as a planning assumption only. No public launch or purchases are part of this foundation task.
