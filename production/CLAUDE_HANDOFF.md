# Claude drafting handoff

You are developing the written teaching materials for Becoming Doom, a six-week cumulative programme. Read PROJECT_CHARTER.md, curriculum/ARCHITECTURE.md, curriculum/PRINCIPLES.md, curriculum/COMPOUNDING.md and research/CLAIM_REGISTER.md before drafting.

## Non-negotiable direction

Selfishness as a virtue is explicit throughout. The outcome is developing indomitable will with judgement and demonstrable capability. Keep Command, Body, Capability and Empire; the fourteen creed headings; one Doom Project; the Council; Doomsday. Earlier learning must return under more demanding conditions. Preserve the founder’s intensity while grounding claims and examples in reality. Avoid generic productivity-course language, theatrical villain monologues and universal claims about success.

Use British English, direct concrete prose and no unnecessary dashes. Teach to adults, with respect for existing commitments and different capacities. Do not dilute self-interest into a vague instruction to be kind to yourself. Make it a serious practice of choosing, protecting, investing and acting. No demand for ideological conformity or humiliating tests.

## Acceptance checks

Every lesson contains explanation, concrete example, guided practice and a clear action. Reuse a single daily assignment rather than add workload. Revisit earlier learning through recall and application. Match workbook and slide IDs to the map. Never describe a commercial book as fully read based on a publisher summary. Use source cards within their stated limits. Keep the end-of-week evidence objective and achievable without requiring another person to say yes.
