# Authoring templates

## Lesson: Wxx-Lxx

Title; status/version; learner outcome; prior principles recalled; new concept; source IDs and exact bounded claims; misconception; 60-second opening; plain-language explanation; ordinary worked example; contrasting mistaken response; guided practice; daily mission ID; evidence criterion; next retrieval; facilitator notes; slide beats; source/claim review.

## Daily workbook page: Wxx-Dxx

Title and time allowance. Recall without notes. Situation: what happened? Priority: what am I protecting? Pressure: what makes this hard? Response: what will I say/do? Model if relevant: why this one, and its limit? Action completed. Evidence. What changed? What will I correct? Keep responses short enough to leave time for the mission.

## Decision record

Decision and deadline; desired result; options including do nothing; facts; assumptions; resource costs; chosen model and limitation; uncertainty; decision; review date; actual outcome; correction. Scale detail to stakes.

## Boundary record

Request; existing obligations; protected priority; proposed response; predicted reaction; actual response; what I did next; whether I honoured or renegotiated a commitment; learning. A deliberate yes can also demonstrate agency.

## Doom Project charter

Audience; problem; chosen output; six-week scope; success evidence; time/resource limit; dependencies; exclusions; first action; review date. Revenue is optional and project-dependent.

## Weekly review

What I produced; what I protected; an older principle applied; one model used well or poorly; feedback; constraint; correction; next commitment; evidence references. Use demonstrated/revise/not yet evidenced.

## Slide brief

Lesson ID; purpose of each slide; exact on-screen words; visual purpose; speaker-note intent; source note where factual; associated workbook page; production status. Prefer one idea per slide and large readable type. Matte black, ivory text, restrained accent. No copied superhero art needed to explain a principle.
