## Scope and provenance

The First No is W01-L02 and its workbook activity is W01-D03. It is not renamed lesson one. The six-week/forty-two-day core remains current. A separate sixty-one-day visual claim is unresolved; proposed reconciliation must not silently change the programme.

Video research is a new discovery library, not a claim to have recovered every earlier video. Video identity and metadata are checked; full playback, captions and timestamps remain unverified. No full third-party transcripts, films, audio, thumbnails or comic panels are included. The rights register records the limited verified quotation separately from candidate dialogue.

The original image sequence leads visual direction. This public repository contains metadata and teaching source, not the image binaries or finished PowerPoint/PDF files. Those are delivered separately to the creator. No private delivery URLs or identifiers are published. Binary delivery must not be inferred from a manifest entry.
