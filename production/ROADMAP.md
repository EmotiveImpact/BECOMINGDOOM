# Production roadmap

## Stage 0: foundation, this edition

Deliverables: charter, fourteen-principle specification, cumulative matrix, six weekly briefs, all 42 daily missions, ten-model toolkit, assessment, research cards, claim register, production templates, Claude handoff and one sample lesson package. These are foundation drafts ready for review and pilot preparation.

## Stage 1: editorial alignment

Review whether the first week's sample sounds like the founder, whether self-interest remains explicit, whether examples are concrete and whether the pressure progression feels useful. Resolve terminology once, then maintain a decision log. Prioritise the programme promise and week-one experience before expanding prose.

## Stage 2: write a complete week

Claude drafts W01-L01–L03, all seven daily workbook pages, a facilitator Council guide and matching slide outlines from the approved architecture. Codex checks cross-file alignment, workload, evidence and source boundaries. Founder reviews voice and ambition. Complete one week before multiplying a flawed style across six.

## Stage 3: complete the course

Use one file per lesson and one workbook section per week. Planned inventory: 18 lesson scripts; 42 daily pages; six weekly review spreads; onboarding/baseline; model reference cards; project/evidence templates; facilitator notes; final assessment; continuation plan. Slide brief working range: 6–10 slides per main lesson, adjusted to teaching need; final slide count is not locked. Slides are to be produced with Claude unless the founder changes the allocation.

## Stage 4: pilot rehearsal

Run the demo lesson with a few representative adults. Check clarity of self-interest, willingness to try the mission, actual time and examples that confuse or alienate. Revise, then run a small full pilot under the assessment plan. Record what happened before making outcome claims.

## Stage 5: promotion and launch preparation

Use the sample promo as a message test. Ask listeners what the course promises, who it is for and what they expect to do. Then test a truthful invitation with a clear time commitment and concrete deliverables. Compare actual opt-ins and follow-through; no conversion prediction is locked. Resolve price, platform, launch date and cohort capacity before public enrolment.

## Readiness checklist

- Every lesson leads to an existing daily mission and evidence criterion.
- All fourteen principles remain traceable.
- Every earlier thread reappears in later weeks.
- Each introduced model has later retrieval/application opportunities.
- Physical activity is adaptable; recovery is a taught practice.
- Examples distinguish choice, influence and external constraints.
- Full books are not described as read unless actually read.
- Workbook and slides use matching identifiers and wording.
- Pilot findings distinguish observed behaviour from reported feeling.

## Current limitations

No finished video/audio, designed workbook PDF, rendered slide deck, delivery platform or tested sales campaign exists in this edition. The demo contains teaching text and production briefs. Work with Claude has been prepared, not transmitted to an external collaborator.
