# Promo concept: Whose life are you building?

Editorial draft for a roughly 60-second voice-led teaser. Timing and conversion are untested. No audio or video has been produced. Use original imagery and a human-scale story before any grand visual metaphor.

## Voiceover draft

How much of your life is spent making sure nobody is disappointed in you?

You say yes. You stay available. You keep the ambition for later.

Becoming Doom begins with a different decision.

Your life deserves your investment.

For six weeks, choose a mission and start building it. Learn to protect your time, think through difficult decisions, hold a boundary and develop a capability you can demonstrate.

Then take your work into the world. Ask. Test. Learn. Return better prepared.

Each week builds on the last. Each principle has to survive contact with your life.

The ambition is indomitable will: a chosen direction, clear judgement and the ability to keep acting when things become difficult.

Becoming Doom. Become Dangerous.

What will you be able to prove?

## Visual beats

An interrupted work session; an unanswered draft request; a clear calendar boundary; concentrated practice; an imperfect prototype; a consequential conversation; a revised output; final demonstration. Keep the person and their choices central. Black, graphite and ivory; restrained dramatic sound; no invented audience testimonials.

## Message-test questions

What do you think this course asks you to do? Who is it for? What does selfishness mean here? Which phrase interested or repelled you? What would you need to know before joining?

Use the answers to improve clarity. Later enrolment copy needs actual dates, price, delivery format and expectations. No claim that every listener will immediately sign up.
