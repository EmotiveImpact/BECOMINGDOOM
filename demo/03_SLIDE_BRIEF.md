# Demo slide production brief

Lesson W01-L02: The First No. Content brief for Claude; no rendered slide deck has been made. Matte-black background, ivory type, restrained amber accent. Large readable text; one idea per slide. Keep source notes in speaker notes unless the slide itself states a research claim.

| Slide | On-screen copy | Visual purpose | Speaker-note direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The First No | A single deliberate statement | Open with the learner’s last reluctant yes |
| 2 | What did your yes displace? | Two competing calendar blocks | Make the cost concrete using the portfolio example |
| 3 | Your life deserves your investment | One sentence with generous space | Define selfishness as a virtue in the course’s terms |
| 4 | Commitment · Request · Approval pressure | Three clearly separated labels | Explain distinctions without implying every demand is optional |
| 5 | “I can’t help on Thursday. I’ve set that evening aside.” | The response as readable text | Demonstrate calm wording and a possible follow-up |
| 6 | Choose. Write. Rehearse. | Three simple actions | Direct learners to W01-D03; allow the pause |
| 7 | Prediction → Actual response → Next action | Three-step reflection | Explain evidence and privacy-conscious submission |
| 8 | What will you do with the time you protect? | Return to the calendar block | Link the boundary to the Doom Project and tomorrow’s recall |

Accessibility: sufficient contrast, readable type, no information carried by colour alone. Avoid decorative interface clutter. Do not use unlicensed comic panels or character imagery as a substitute for a clear example.
