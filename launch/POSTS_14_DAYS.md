## Day 4 · Wednesday

**Morning, thread**

1/ The first no is easy. The second one is where people fold.

2/ You say "I can't on Thursday." They say "You can move that, surely?" And suddenly you are explaining your entire week and offering Friday.

3/ Three rules for the follow-up.

4/ Repeat, do not escalate. "I'm keeping that time. Thursday won't work for me." Same words, same tone.

5/ Drop the defence. Every extra reason is a new thing for them to argue with.

6/ Only offer an alternative you actually want to give. A resented alternative is just a delayed yes.

7/ The full lesson, with a workbook to rehearse your own follow-up, is free. Comment NO.

## Day 10 · Tuesday

**Morning**

A freelancer agrees to a fourth unpaid revision. Their own portfolio has not been touched in three months.

Option 1: "Sure, no problem." Resentment, and the portfolio slips another month.
Option 2: "This is ridiculous, I'm done." Burns a client over something fixable.
Option 3: "The quote covered two rounds. I'm happy to do a third at my standard rate, or we can finalise this version."

Option 3 is clear, fair and keeps the time that belongs to your own work.

**Evening**

Name the label you have been carrying that you never tested. "I'm not a business person." "I'm not creative." Then design the smallest experiment that would prove it wrong.
