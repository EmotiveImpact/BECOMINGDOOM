## UK consumer law note

Buyers of online services in the UK normally have a 14-day right to cancel. Because the course starts within 14 days of purchase, the checkout should ask buyers to confirm they want the service to begin before the cancellation period ends and acknowledge that they may then pay for what they have used. The week one refund promise above is more generous than this anyway, but take advice if unsure.
